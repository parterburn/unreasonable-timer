A closer match to the web timer, and a cleaner window while presenting.

- Pick an accent color in the setup form or in Settings. Teal stays the default; every glow, gradient, button and the Dock icon follow your choice.
- Hold ↑ or ↓ to keep adding or removing time instead of pressing repeatedly. Holding ↓ stops just above zero, so you can't run into "time is up" by accident.
- The countdown's glow and edge vignette now fill the whole window, with no band under the title bar.
- While a timer runs and the mouse is still, the window buttons fade out along with the cursor.
- The setup form no longer opens with a blue selection over the minutes, shows every field label, and has the web page's tinted hourglass.
- Version numbers are now correct, so future versions can update themselves.
