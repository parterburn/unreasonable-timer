Zoom, a clickable progress bar, and new chimes.

- Zoom the countdown with ⌘+ and ⌘- (or ⌘=), and ⌘0 for actual size. Zooming in stops at the largest size that still fits the window.
- Click or drag along the progress bar to jump to that point in the countdown. Hovering shows the time you'd jump to.
- Hold ↑ or ↓ to keep adding or removing time. Holding ↓ stops just above zero, so you can't run into "time is up" by accident.
- The last ten seconds now pulse gently in size each second instead of flashing color.
- With no text showing above or below, the digits sit in the middle of the window.
- Choose the chime sound in Settings: Classic, Singing bowl or Marimba, with a button to preview each one. The singing bowl rings three times at zero, about ten seconds apart.
- Settings shows the app's version next to Check Now.
