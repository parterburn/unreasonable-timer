A native setup screen.

- The setup screen now looks and works like System Settings. Saved and recent timers are in a sidebar, and the timer's options are grouped below a live preview of the countdown.
- Choose a duration with the minute and second fields, or with one click on 1, 3, 5, 10, 15, 30 or 60 minutes.
- Select a saved timer to edit it, then Update to keep the change. Double-click any timer in the sidebar to open it right away. Right-click to start or delete one.
- The setup screen follows your Mac's light or dark appearance. A timer's own theme still sets how its countdown looks, and the preview shows it.
- Unreasonable's colors lead the accent choices: Unreasonable Teal, Unreasonable Impact, Unreasonable Food and Eco Purple. Point at a color to see its name. An accent now shows exactly as picked.
- Accent color and chime sound now belong to each timer, so a keynote can ring a singing bowl in violet while a lightning talk uses the classic chime in teal. Both moved from Settings to the setup screen, with buttons to hear the chimes. Each timer can also chime at 15 seconds left, or stay silent. Existing timers start out teal with the classic chime.
- At zero the countdown eases into the overtime style, without the flash of color. The text for zero shows exactly as you typed it, no longer forced into capitals.
- In the last ten seconds, each second gives a quick pulse of the accent color as well as the gentle swell.
- After time is up, a click or Space no longer resets the timer straight away. It asks first, and a second press within a few seconds resets. R still resets at once.
- Settings has a small "Made by people + 🤖 from Unreasonable" line at the bottom.
- Update fixes: older versions in the update feed now point at their own downloads, and the app no longer tries a missing partial download before the full one.
