Settings and the setup screen on one page, and a right-click menu on the timer.

- The preview of the countdown stays at the top of the setup screen while you edit. It is the real countdown scaled down, so text wraps as it will on screen, and it follows the field you're in: the start, 15 seconds left with the warning text, or time's up with the zero text, overtime and people waiting.
- The setup screen and Settings grow with the window instead of stopping at a narrow column.
- Settings are one page in the main window: click the gear at the bottom left, or press ⌘,. Done takes you back to the timer you were editing. While a timer is on screen, ⌘, opens them in their own window so the countdown keeps going.
- One global shortcut: Show Timer, in Settings ▸ App. Click the box, press the keys you want (⌃⌥⌘T, say), and that shortcut brings the timer forward from any app, even after its window was closed. The global shortcuts for start, reset and ±15 seconds are gone; those keys work whenever the timer is in front, and Shortcuts.app can still start or pause a timer.
- Right-click the countdown for a menu of everything it can do (start, pause, reset, ±15 seconds, zoom, fullscreen, edit), each with its key. The hint line under the timer is gone.
- Esc goes back to editing the timer, after leaving fullscreen if it's fullscreen. While the timer is counting it asks first, and a second Esc confirms.
- Unreasonable's colors lead the accent choices: Unreasonable Teal, Unreasonable Impact, Unreasonable Food and Eco Purple. Point at a color to see its name. An accent now shows exactly as picked.
- The button that opens the countdown is called Open Timer. "Number of people in the room" and "Count up after zero" say what they do.
- The menu bar menu has Check for Updates, and its Settings item brings the app forward.
- The duration fields no longer show their name beside the digits.
- Going back to the setup screen after a dark-themed countdown no longer leaves the window half dark with white bands at the sides.
