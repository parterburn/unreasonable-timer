Text sizes for each timer, and arrow keys in the time fields.

- Each timer sets its own sizes: the timer, the text under it, the text at 15 seconds left, the text at zero and the people waiting line. Each has a percentage beside it on the setup screen (50% to 200%, in steps of 10), and the preview jumps to the moment you're sizing. Right-click a percentage to put it back to 100%. If the sizes add up to more than the window can hold, the countdown shrinks just enough to fit, so nothing is pushed off screen.
- In the minutes, seconds and number of people fields, ↑ and ↓ step the number; hold to keep going. Seconds carry into minutes, so 0:59 goes to 1:00.
- The Start / 15 seconds left / Time is up tabs under the preview are full size and as wide as the preview, like the tabs in System Settings.
